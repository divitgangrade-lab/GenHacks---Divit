You are shopping. The page says "biodegradable", "climate neutral", "eco-friendly". Since 27 September 2026, Directive (EU) 2024/825 bans those claims unless the seller can prove them.

Claim Check is a bookmark. Drag it to the bookmarks bar, click it on any product page, and a panel opens on the page with every claim it finds: the sentence quoted, the phrase marked, the rule and the article that apply, and the evidence that would make the claim lawful.

Try it: https://divitgangrade-lab.github.io/GenHacks---Divit/
Source: https://github.com/divitgangrade-lab/GenHacks---Divit

Nothing is uploaded. The page text never leaves the browser, which is what makes it safe to run on a checkout page.

The shop page in this film is synthetic — written for the demo, and labelled as such on screen. The field run shown is 13 saved pages, checked on 21 November 2026: 3 with a banned claim, 0 false positives across 3 reviewed. It is a pilot, not a survey: small, brand-skewed, six pages from outside the EU. English pages, EU law only; it reads wording, never product facts, and it sees no images. Not legal advice.
